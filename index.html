About Our School

A Government Boys Higher Secondary School in Bhansinghabad, Mirpurkhas

Govt. Boys Higher Secondary School Bhansinghabad Mirpurkhas, Sindh, has served the community for over 20 years and today educates 864 students from Class 6 to Class 12 with the help of 33 teachers. We are committed to academic development, discipline, character building and the growth of every student. SEMIS Code: 405030386

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable education that builds strong academic foundations, discipline and good character, and prepares every student for higher studies and responsible citizenship.

Our Vision

To be a leading government school in Mirpurkhas, producing confident, skilled and ethical young men who contribute positively to Sindh and Pakistan.

Our Values

Integrity, respect, discipline, hard work, teamwork and responsibility toward family, school and society.
